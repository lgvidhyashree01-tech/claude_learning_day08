Executive Summary
Across 8 major Indian metro cities analyzed in June 2026, the average AQI stands at 75 — in the Moderate range, largely aided by the monsoon season lowering dust particulates. Bangalore leads as the cleanest city (AQI 43, Good), benefiting from its altitude, green cover, and post-rain air washing. Delhi remains the most polluted (AQI 100) even in the "off-season" for pollution, driven by chronic vehicular and industrial emissions. Chennai sits at AQI 68 — Moderate — with improvement due to recent coastal winds and light drizzle, but faces persistent issues from the Manali industrial corridor and high water hardness (TDS 300–600 ppm). The coastal location provides natural air scrubbing but also contributes to hard, mineral-heavy tap water affecting skin and hair health significantly.
⚠ Biggest Anomaly Detected
Manali, Chennai recorded PM10 AQI of 999 (Hazardous) — a stark outlier within the same city that averages AQI 68 overall. The industrial zone in north Chennai (refineries, petrochemical plants, Ennore Port) creates a hyper-local pollution hotspot that is 14× worse than the city average. This localized hazard is invisible in city-level summaries but poses severe respiratory risk to ~200,000 residents in that zone.

📝 Detailed Health Notes
Lungs: At AQI 68, Chennai's air is acceptable for most. However, PM2.5 at ~18 µg/m³ (3.6× WHO limit) causes mild airway irritation on prolonged outdoor exposure. Sensitive individuals may notice slight wheeze or throat dryness.
Sleep: Indoor PM infiltration at night reduces sleep quality by ~8–12% at current levels. Keeping windows closed and using fans rather than open ventilation after 10pm is advised.
Hard Water (TDS 300–600 ppm): Chennai's tap water is significantly harder than WHO standards (TDS ≤ 300 ppm). High calcium and magnesium strip natural hair oils, cause breakage, and leave mineral deposits on skin — worsening dryness and blocking pores. Borewell areas (OMR, Tambaram, Velachery) can hit 1000+ ppm TDS — among India's worst for hair and skin.
Exercise: Morning jogs before 8am and after 6pm are recommended. Current AQI poses moderate risk during intense outdoor exercise (running, cycling). A mask is optional but advisable near GST Road or OMR during rush hours.

💡 Surprising Observation
Delhi AQI is 100 in June — its "good season." During winter (Dec–Feb), Delhi regularly hits AQI 300–500. The gap between Delhi's best (summer, 70–100) and worst (winter, 300–600+) is the largest of any Indian metro — a 5–8× seasonal swing driven by crop burning, temperature inversions, and halted monsoon wind patterns.
🌊 Chennai Water Insight
Chennai has some of India's hardest tap water. Borewell areas (OMR, Tambaram, Velachery) regularly exceed 1,000 ppm TDS — 3× WHO safe limits. IIT Madras found E.coli in 75% of Chennai homes. Despite decent air quality, water quality makes Chennai one of the harder cities for skin and hair health in South India.

Tailored for Chennai residents — AQI 68, Hard Water TDS 300–600 ppm

🌅
Daily Actions
Check AQI each morning at AQI.in before outdoor activities
Exercise before 8am or after 6pm to avoid peak pollution hours
Keep windows closed between 10pm–6am when AQI spikes indoors
Drink 3–4L of filtered/RO water daily — tap water is unsafe
Rinse face after outdoor time to remove PM2.5 deposits
🏠
Indoor Air Improvements
Place spider plants, peace lilies, or snake plants — natural PM absorbers
Use a HEPA air purifier in bedroom (PM2.5 filtration ≥ 99%)
Vacuum with HEPA filter weekly to trap particulate dust
Avoid incense sticks and mosquito coils — they spike indoor PM2.5 to 300+
Use exhaust fans during cooking — Indian cooking generates PM2.5 peaks
💇
Hair Care (Hard Water)
Install a shower filter (TDS-reducing) — essential for OMR, Tambaram, Velachery areas
Use a chelating shampoo (EDTA-based) weekly to remove mineral buildup
Apple cider vinegar rinse (1:4 dilution) after washing to remove calcium deposits
Apply leave-in conditioner after every wash — hard water strips moisture
Oil massage (coconut/sesame) twice weekly to counter mineral damage
✨
Skin Care
Double cleanse at night — micellar water first to lift PM2.5, then face wash
Use SPF 30+ daily — UV index in Chennai averages 8–11 (Extreme)
Apply a barrier cream or ceramide moisturizer after bathing (hard water dehydrates)
Vitamin C serum helps combat oxidative stress from PM2.5 exposure
Use a mild pH-balanced cleanser (5.5) to counter hard water's alkaline damage
💧
Water Quality Fixes
Install RO + UV purifier for drinking — mandatory given E.coli contamination
Add a shower filter (KDF or carbon + calcium sulfite media) at ₹1,500–4,000
Test home TDS with a meter (₹200 online) — know your exact hardness level
Do not use unfiltered tap water for cooking or brushing teeth
Consider a whole-house softener if TDS > 800 ppm (borewell areas)
🏃
Outdoor Activity Guidance
Safe for outdoor runs: before 8am when AQI typically dips to 40–55
Wear N95 if jogging near GST Road, OMR IT Corridor, or Manali area
Marina Beach walk is recommended — sea breeze typically gives AQI 30–45
Avoid outdoor exercise on days AQI > 100 — check app before heading out
Stay hydrated outdoors — Chennai's humidity causes rapid dehydration
